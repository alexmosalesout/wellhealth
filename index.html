01 · Category

A category in inflection.

Longevity, metabolic health, hormone optimization, and prevention have moved from fringe to mainstream consumer healthcare.

i.

Demand has changed.

Patients are seeking proactive, physician-led care — body composition, metabolic risk, hormones, early detection — well before disease appears.

ii.

Supply has not kept pace.

The traditional system is built around episodic, reactive care. Few providers are organized around longitudinal, biomarker-driven optimization.

iii.

Adjacent categories validate it.

GLP-1 adoption, hormone therapy growth, and consumer interest in DEXA, VO₂, CGM, and preventive imaging signal a durable shift in willingness to pay.

04 · Compliance & Model

A compliant hybrid model. Insured and uninsured pathways.

Substantial internal work has gone into structuring MVC and HVC as a physician-led operating model that cleanly separates publicly insured care from privately funded optimization services.

MSP-Insured Stream

Medically indicated care

Recently introduced in response to patient demand for medically indicated assessments. Operates within standard MSP billing rules, clinical scope, and CPSBC expectations.

Private Membership Stream

Optimization, longevity, performance

Membership care remains focused on uninsured services: optimization, longevity, performance, DEXA, VO₂ max, advanced wellness planning, and prevention-oriented coaching.

Full separation across documentation, consent, billing, and patient communication. BC's Medicare Protection Act and CPSBC guidance impose meaningful constraints on physician-led private care — the model is built to preserve compliance while improving access, continuity, and scalability.
